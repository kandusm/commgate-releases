CommGate 0.5.0-alpha.12: licenses of the engine and the Control4 drivers
==========================================================================

The engine tarballs (commgate-engine-0.5.0-alpha.12-linux-arm64.tar.gz and -linux-x64.tar.gz), the gateway software
they install under /opt/commgate (also in the hardware image), and the Control4 drivers
(commgate-drivers-0.5.0-alpha.12.zip and the eight .c4z files) contain no software licensed under the GPL or the LGPL.
No source offer applies to them.

What they contain:

* CommVergent's own code: the engine, its installer and scripts, and the eight drivers. Proprietary. The engine and
  the CommGate gateway image are licensed under the CommGate Engine License Agreement, EULA.txt: those are the
  engine's governing terms. The drivers are licensed separately through DriverCentral.
* Open-source dependencies of the engine, all under permissive licenses: matter.js (@matter/*, Apache-2.0),
  @noble/curves and @noble/hashes (MIT), and ws (MIT). Their license and NOTICE texts are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.
* The Node.js runtime (node/bin/node), MIT-licensed. Its node/LICENSE also carries the notices of the libraries
  built into it. The GPL texts in that file belong to build scripts of one of those libraries (ICU's autoconf
  macros, under the Autoconf exception), which aren't part of the runtime.
* In the parent driver (commgate.c4z) only: DriverCentral's licensing client, proprietary software of DriverCentral
  LLC, used under its Developer Agreement.

The hardware image also contains Raspberry Pi OS packages, many under the GPL or LGPL: see SOURCE_OFFER.txt.
